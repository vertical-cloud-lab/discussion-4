# Purple Butterfly with "DRIVERS" — Design

A text-only (ASCII) design for a purple paper butterfly with the word
**DRIVERS** written in ALL CAPS across the lower wings, plus a yellow pipe
cleaner threaded through two small diamond cutouts on the upper wings to
form the antennae.

> Color note: The construction paper used is **PURPLE**, and the threading
> material is a **YELLOW** pipe cleaner. Because this design must be
> text-only, the colors are documented here rather than rendered.

## The Design (ASCII)

```
              \           /
               \   ___   /         <- yellow pipe cleaner antennae, bent up
                \_/   \_/             out of the upper-wing diamonds
        ___    /       \    ___
       /   \__/  <>   <>  \__/   \   <- two diamond cutouts on upper wings
      /                          \      (pipe cleaner threaded through both)
     |       D R I V E R S        |
      \                          /
       \__                    __/
          \__              __/
             \__        __/
                \      /
                 |    |              <- center body, vertical fold line
                 |    |
                 |____|
```

The label `DRIVERS` sits horizontally on a single line across the lower
wings just below the body's midpoint, in all capital letters and
letter-spaced so it spans nearly the full wingspan with a small whitespace
margin on either side. Two small diamond cutouts sit symmetrically on the
upper wings, one on each side of the body, used as threading holes for the
yellow pipe cleaner. The pipe cleaner is threaded **up through the left
diamond and back down through the right diamond** so the visible portion
above the wings forms two short antennae bent gently upward and outward.

## Role Assignments

There are four roles. Each person owns a distinct part of the build so the
sample can move smoothly from one to the next.

| Role       | Tool / Responsibility                | Task on this project                                                                 |
| ---------- | ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Folder** | Folds the paper                      | Folds the purple paper in half vertically so the butterfly is left-right symmetric when cut. |
| **Marker** | Writes / draws                       | Draws the half-butterfly outline on the folded edge, marks the upper-wing diamond cutout, and writes **DRIVERS** across the lower wings. |
| **Cutter** | Holds the scissors                   | Cuts along the outline and the diamond cutout, unfolds the finished butterfly, and threads the yellow pipe cleaner through the two upper-wing diamonds to form the antennae. |
| **Bus**    | Moves the sample between teammates   | Carries the sample forward only: Folder → Marker → Cutter → final display. The flow is one-way; the Bus never returns the sample to a previous step. |

## Steps

The pipeline is **one-way**: each role finishes its work and hands the
sample forward via the Bus. The sample never returns to a previous step,
so each role must complete its task correctly before passing it on.

1. **Folder** takes a sheet of purple paper and folds it in half (left edge
   to right edge) so the fold becomes the butterfly's center body, then
   hands it off (still folded).
2. **Bus** carries the folded paper forward from the Folder to the Marker.
3. **Marker** draws the half-butterfly outline against the folded edge
   (upper wing, lower wing, and body), marks one small diamond cutout on
   the upper wing (which will mirror across the fold to two diamonds when
   unfolded), and notes on the back where **DRIVERS** will be written
   across the lower wings — horizontally on a single line in all capital
   letters, letter-spaced to span nearly the full wingspan with a small
   whitespace margin on either side.
4. **Bus** carries the marked, folded paper forward from the Marker to the
   Cutter.
5. **Cutter** cuts along the outline drawn by the Marker (keeping the fold
   intact so the butterfly stays symmetric), cuts out the marked diamond
   on the upper wing, unfolds the finished butterfly, threads the yellow
   pipe cleaner up through the left upper-wing diamond and back down
   through the right upper-wing diamond so the visible portion above the
   wings forms two short antennae bent gently upward and outward, and
   writes **DRIVERS** across the lower wings following the placement noted
   by the Marker.
6. **Bus** carries the finished butterfly forward from the Cutter to the
   final display, where the purple butterfly with **DRIVERS** across the
   lower wings and yellow pipe cleaner antennae is shown.
