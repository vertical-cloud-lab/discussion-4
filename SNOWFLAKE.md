# Blue Snowflake with "SOFTWARE" — Design

A text-only (ASCII) design for a blue paper snowflake with the word
**SOFTWARE** written in ALL CAPS across the middle of the snowflake.

> Color note: The construction paper used is **BLUE**. Because this design
> must be text-only, the color is documented here rather than rendered.

## The Design (ASCII)

```
                *
                |
            *   |   *
              \ | /
                +
           ____/ \____
   *------/   S O F T  \------*
          \   W A R E  /
           ‾‾‾‾\ /‾‾‾‾
                +
              / | \
            *   |   *
                |
                *
```

The label `SOFTWARE` sits across the horizontal axis of the snowflake on
two short rows (`S O F T` above, `W A R E` below), in all capital letters,
letter-spaced so the word stays legible against the radial cuts. The label
is centered on the snowflake's center point so it reads correctly when the
snowflake is held with any of its six points up. The six points and the
radial cuts give the design six-fold symmetry.

## Role Assignments

There are four roles. Each person owns a distinct part of the build so the
sample can move smoothly from one to the next.

| Role       | Tool / Responsibility                | Task on this project                                                                 |
| ---------- | ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Folder** | Folds the paper                      | Folds the blue paper into a six-fold wedge (in half, then into thirds) so the snowflake is six-way symmetric when cut. |
| **Marker** | Writes / draws                       | Draws the cut pattern on the folded wedge and writes **SOFTWARE** across the middle of the unfolded snowflake. |
| **Cutter** | Holds the scissors                   | Cuts along the pattern drawn by the Marker and unfolds the finished snowflake.       |
| **Bus**    | Moves the sample between teammates   | Carries the paper forward only: Folder → Marker → Cutter → final display. The flow is one-way; the Bus never returns the sample to a previous step. |

## Steps

The pipeline is **one-way**: each role finishes its work and hands the sample
forward via the Bus. The sample never returns to a previous step, so each
role must complete its task correctly before passing it on.

1. **Folder** takes a square sheet of blue paper, folds it in half along the
   diagonal, then folds the resulting triangle into thirds to produce a
   narrow six-fold wedge, then hands it off (still folded).
2. **Bus** carries the folded wedge forward from the Folder to the Marker.
3. **Marker** draws the cut pattern (notches and shapes) along the edges of
   the folded wedge, then notes on the back where **SOFTWARE** will be
   written across the middle once the snowflake is unfolded, in all capital
   letters with the letters split across two short rows above and below the
   center as shown in the design above.
4. **Bus** carries the marked, folded wedge forward from the Marker to the
   Cutter.
5. **Cutter** cuts along the pattern drawn by the Marker, unfolds the
   finished snowflake, and writes **SOFTWARE** across the middle following
   the placement noted by the Marker.
6. **Bus** carries the finished snowflake forward from the Cutter to the
   final display, where the blue snowflake with **SOFTWARE** across the
   middle is shown.
